## Projects (concept studies)

Her own prototypes showing how Aida thinks about HR processes. They are concept studies, not products in use. They run in the browser with sample data and fictional people.

- [Vacation Handover (Urlaubsübergabe)](https://aidadulic.at/urlaubsuebergabe/): publicly available demo, in German. Problem: responsibilities and open items get lost before vacation. Approach: a guided handover with deputy, priorities and checklist. Benefit: fewer follow-up questions, clear responsibility during absence.
- Wissen bleibt ("Knowledge stays"): concept study on securing knowledge when people leave or change roles. Shown only in a personal meeting, not publicly available.
- Exit-Coach: concept study on preparing separation and termination conversations. Shown only in a personal meeting, not publicly available.

Note: Aida built the prototypes and the demo video herself with AI. The video "Urlaubsübergabe in 2 Minuten" (working title "Vacation Hub") is presented by her AI avatar; it is not a real video of her. The prototypes store input only locally in the visitor's browser.
